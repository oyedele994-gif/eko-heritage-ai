Iya Alaro: A Life Remembered

Safiyat Ogun Dupe, remembered by many as Iya Alaro. Explore the family account of her life, work, community connections and enduring legacy.

About this documentary

This film is presented alongside the recorded family biography. It covers her origins in Osun State, family life, herbal trading, her connection with Amoo Street and the community memories associated with Iya Alaro Bridge.

The bridge stories, traditional practices and reported age are preserved as family and community oral history, not independently verified facts.

About Ajeran

Ajeran is remembered as an ancestral Yoruba masquerade tradition connected with Ojota. Community accounts speak of four forefathers, including Chief Gafar, and of a tradition carried through generations.

The masquerade's distinctive cultural appearance includes colorful red, royal-blue and golden-yellow elements, patterned coverings and a horsetail whisk.

Photographs & cultural materials

The Ajeran exhibition will bring together the seven community photographs already featured in the museum, historical explanations and related cultural materials.

These materials help visitors understand the tradition and the importance of preserving local heritage.
